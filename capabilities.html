Capabilities

What we can make for you

CNC machining, metal fabrication, and prototyping under one roof, for parts that have to fit, function, and pass inspection.

01

CNC Machining

Precision milled and turned parts in metals and engineering plastics, programmed from your CAD model or 2D drawing.

  • CNC milling of prismatic parts, pockets, and complex features
  • CNC turning of shafts, bushings, pins, and fittings
  • Drilling, tapping, threading, and reaming
  • Deburring and edge-break to your drawing notes
  • Tolerances held to your print. Tell us what you need.

02

Metal Fabrication

Fabricated parts and assemblies built from plate, sheet, tube, and bar stock.

  • Cutting, sawing, and forming
  • Welding and weldment assemblies
  • Brackets, mounts, frames, and enclosures
  • Jigs, fixtures, and shop tooling
  • Machining after welding for critical features

03

Prototyping

Get a working part in your hands quickly, then refine it. We help you go from first article to a part that is ready for production.

  • One-off prototypes and short runs
  • Design-for-manufacturability feedback on your drawing
  • Fast iteration as your design changes
  • Bridge production until your volume tooling is ready
