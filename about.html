About us

An engineer-run shop in Arlington, Texas

Baria Manufacturing makes precision machined and fabricated parts for companies that need them made right and delivered when promised.

Who we are

Built on clear communication

Baria Manufacturing LLC was founded in 2026 in Arlington, Texas, in the heart of the Dallas–Fort Worth aerospace and automotive corridor. We provide CNC machining, metal fabrication, and prototyping for customers who value precision and straight answers.

When you send us a drawing, an engineer reviews it. If something on the print is unclear or could be made more cost-effectively, we'll tell you before we cut metal, not after.
